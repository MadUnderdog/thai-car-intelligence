## 5. The one rule this wave adds — and what it deliberately does not do

`price_row_boundary(nxt, body)` in `scripts/p106_variant_recovery.py`:

- a **published price may delimit a name row** — either a price-token line
  (`฿` / `THB` / `บาท` / `ราคา` / `เริ่มต้น`), or a bare figure
  (`^[0-9][0-9,.]{4,}$`, ≥6 digits) **only when the page publishes prices**;
- it is a boundary, never a value: **no price is read, stored or verified**
  (`price_pass: false`; `test_price_is_boundary_only_and_never_stored` rejects any
  price key and any baht figure inside evidence, and rejects monthly figures like
  `23,799` and years like `2026` as boundaries);
- everything else is unchanged from the accepted P105 REPAIR-2 behaviour:
  `page_model_set()` dedicated/aggregate/unbound binding, cross-page rejection,
  `grade_label_blocker()` promotion/percent/numeric-label class, nav/footer/meta
  detection, generic-label list, deterministic `sorted()` tie-breaking.

## 7. Why the other zero-gain OEMs stayed flat (audited, not hidden)

- **Mazda (33):** the committed spec/car pages publish trims only inside prose
  (`ในรุ่น Prime`, `รุ่น XDL Signature`) — prose is not a grade row; the CX-30
  page still publishes `2.0 Prime` for `Mazda Cx-3`, which P105 REPAIR-1 rejects
  as cross-page/promotion-class.  The new capture path `/th/cars` returned 404.
- **Nissan (24):** the official grade cards publish *prefixed* grade text
  (`e-POWER V *`, `1.0L TURBO E CVT`, `KC Calibre SL 7AT`) that is not the
  published form of the candidate labels (`E`, `EL`, `V`, `VL`, `SV`); other
  candidates are attributed to a different model entirely
  (`Nissan GT R | CALIBRE E 6MT`, `Nissan GT R | SL 6MT 4WD`).  Confirming either
  would be inference or cross-model binding, so they stay IDENTITY_ONLY.
- **Porsche (21):** attempted Thai model paths (`/thailand/models/...`) 404;
  committed pages (home, press, Macan) do not publish the candidate labels, and
  `Porsche Macan | น้ำมัน / รถไฟฟ้า` is fuel-type-as-variant enumerator noise.
- **Subaru (9):** `lineup.php` exists only for Forester (404 for BRZ/Crosstrek);
  the committed BRZ page does not publish `2.0 6AT` / `2.0 6MT` as rows; the
  Crosstrek page publishes `2.0 iS Eyesight` for `Crosstrek`, not for the `XV`
  candidates (cross-model → rejected).
- **MINI (7):** the official price sheet heads sections with `MINI COOPER.` /
  `MINI COUNTRYMAN.` while the candidates are recorded under `MINI HATCH`;
  `MINI COUNTRYMAN S ALL4 - HIGHTRIM` appears once per section with no price row
  and no second distinct grade, so the grade-list requirement is not met.
- **Changan (2):** `Lumin L DC` rows are followed by CTA text
  (`โปรโมชัน`, `คำนวนค่างวด`), not a published price, and the candidate `L` is
  never published as a standalone row.
- **Jaguar (2):** the price sheet sections (`F-TYPE`, `I-PACE`) do not match the
  candidate model names (`JAGUAR F-TYPE 2.0 Liter Ingenium Petrol Coupe`), so no
  same-model binding exists.
- **Kia leftover `LIGHT`:** present in the body but never as a standalone grade
  row → stays IDENTITY_ONLY.
